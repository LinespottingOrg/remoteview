# Remote View

A course in remote viewing: the Star Gate record, the six-stage method, spoken lectures, and a blind practice lab.

app.remoteview · Linespotting

Eight lectures with transcripts and short films. The archive keeps the 1995 split — Jessica Utts, Ray Hyman, and the American Institutes for Research — rather than a single slogan. The lab is a sealed practice page: ideogram, senses, sketch, then feedback and a score that is allowed to be zero.

This is an educational reconstruction of a published method and the public research record. It is not medicine, not legal evidence, and not a tool for viewing people.

Primary sources are linked inside the Record: the CIA STARGATE collection, Targ & Puthoff in *Nature* (1974), and the 1995 evaluations.
